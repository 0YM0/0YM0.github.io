👋 About Me

I am a B.S. student in Applied Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University of Science and Technology (SeoulTech). My research interests lie in computer vision, robot vision, and visual perception, particularly in multi-modal learning and vision-language models.

My research experience has focused on visual perception and multi-modal learning, primarily using satellite imagery as an application domain. I am currently interested in extending my research toward robot vision.
